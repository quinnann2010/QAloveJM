    JANUARY 1, 2027 · FROM ME TO YOU

    Happy New Year, my love! ✨

    My dearest Jarod,

    Happy New Year, my love! ♡

    Before we say hello to 2027, I want to say one last little thank you to 2026.

    Thank you, 2026, for all the unexpected turns, the little surprises, and especially for bringing you into my life.

    And thank you, universe, for somehow making our paths cross. Out of all the people in this big, big world, you brought me to you. I don't know how you did it, but I think that was a pretty lovely decision. ♡

    If someone had told me at the beginning of 2026 that I'd be ending the year with a boyfriend who makes me smile at my phone like a silly little girl, I probably wouldn't have believed them. But here we are!

    I don't know what 2027 has waiting for us, and that's actually one of the things I'm most excited about.

    There are so many places we haven't explored, so many things we haven't tried, so many kisses I haven't stolen, and so many ordinary little days that haven't become our favourite memories yet.

    I want more dates, more silly jokes, more cooking experiments, more cuddles, more sunsets, and more moments when we look at each other and think, ‘I'm so glad it's you.’

    Of course, I know not every day will be sunshine and butterflies. There might be difficult days, misunderstandings, and moments when we drive each other a little crazy. But I hope we'll always find our way back to each other, with patience, kindness, and lots and lots of love.

    My little wish for 2027 is simple: I hope we keep growing together, discovering new things about each other, and choosing each other, even on the ordinary days.

    And maybe I'm being a little greedy, but I don't want just one more year with you.

    I want many, many more.

    I want to collect New Year's memories with you until we lose count. I want us to look back one day and laugh at how young and silly we were when we celebrated our very first New Year together.

    So, dear universe, if you're listening, thank you for bringing him to me. Please let us have a whole lot more chapters together. ♡

    Here's to 2027, to all the beautiful things we haven't experienced yet, and to us.

    Happy New Year, my favourite person.

    With all my love, a thousand kisses, and a very hopeful little heart,

    Your little girlfriend,

    Quỳnh Anh ♡
