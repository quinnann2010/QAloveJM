Our First Christmas ♡

To Jarod, from your little girl

✦ ♡ ✦

My dearest Jarod,

Can you believe this is our very first Christmas together? And actually… it's my first time really celebrating Christmas, too. 🥺🎄

I know that sounds a little funny, because I studied in Canada, where Christmas was everywhere — the snow, the decorations, the lights, all of it. But I grew up with Eastern traditions, so Christmas was never really a holiday I celebrated myself. I mostly watched the magic happen around me.

And now, somehow, my first real Christmas gets to be with you. That makes this little day feel extra special to me. ♡

I don't know what all our future Christmases will look like, but I really, really hope this is only the first of many. I hope we'll get to make our own silly traditions, share warm hugs on cold days, laugh at little things, and keep choosing to spend these moments together.

So here's a tiny piece of Christmas magic from your little girl. It's not a big present, but I made it with a whole lot of love — just for you.

With a million little kisses,
Your little girl, Quỳnh Anh 💗
