# ♡ A Little Love, Written in Code

*The little love story of an IT girl who doesn't always know how to express her feelings, so sometimes, she turns them into something you can click, play with, and smile at.*

---

## 🌷 Once Upon a Time...

There was a little girl who studied IT.

She spent most of her days surrounded by computers, assignments, complicated algorithms, and lines of code that didn't always work the way she wanted.

And then, one day, she met a boy.

A boy who somehow became someone she wanted to share her little world with.

Now, this girl wasn't exactly an expert at expressing all her feelings. Sometimes, she had too many thoughts in her head and couldn't find the right words.

But there was one thing she knew how to do.

**She could make things.**

So she decided to turn some of her feelings into little projects.

Not because she had to.

But simply because thinking about him made her want to create something special.

## 💌 What Is This Little Place?

This is a collection of tiny surprises made by that little IT girl for her favorite boy.

You don't need to understand programming, darling.

You don't need to know what HTML, CSS, or JavaScript means.

All you need to do is open a project, click around, explore, and hopefully smile a little.

Some surprises might be romantic.

Some might be silly.

Some might make you laugh, and some might make you wonder what on earth is going on inside your girlfriend's head.

But they all have one thing in common:

**Every single one was made with you in mind. ♡**

## 🧸 A Few Things You Should Know

This little world might not always be perfect.

Sometimes, a button might not work.

Sometimes, something might look a little strange.

And sometimes, the girl behind it might spend hours fixing one tiny detail that you'll probably never notice.

But that's okay.

Because perfection was never the point.

**Making something that could bring a smile to your face was.**
